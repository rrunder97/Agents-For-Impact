📚 Official Government & Federal Data Sources (7)

All primary data sources are publicly available, official government datasets actively maintained by federal institutions.

1

FEMA National Shelter System (NSS)

FEMA & American Red Cross

Standing national inventory of registered emergency evacuation shelters, capacity metrics, and operational flags (wheelchair access, generators, pets).

Public & Maintained ArcGIS Feature Service (gis.fema.gov)
2

HHS emPOWER Program

U.S. Dept of Health & Human Services (ASPR / CMS)

Monthly datasets tracking Medicare beneficiaries who rely on electricity-dependent life-maintaining medical equipment (ventilators, dialysis, oxygen).

Public & Maintained Privacy Protected (counts 1–10 masked as "11")
3

American Community Survey (ACS)

U.S. Census Bureau

Demographic characteristics, total households, zero-vehicle households, and housing infrastructure metrics at the census tract level.

Public & Maintained Google Cloud Public Datasets (bigquery-public-data.census_bureau_acs)
4

CDC Social Vulnerability Index (SVI)

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC / ATSDR)

Tract-level percentile rankings assessing socioeconomic vulnerability, household composition, minority status, and housing/transportation barriers.

Public & Maintained ArcGIS REST Endpoints (Updated biennially)
5

CDC PLACES (Local Data for Health)

CDC in partnership with CDC Foundation

Model-based small-area health estimates (walking difficulties, independent living capabilities, cognitive disability) at census tract resolution.

Public & Maintained Open Data API (data.cdc.gov)
6

FEMA National Risk Index (NRI)

Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA)

Baseline natural hazard risk measurements, annualized loss estimates, and exposure metrics for hurricanes, coastal flooding, and severe weather.

Public & Maintained ArcGIS Services (FEMA Open Data)
7

CMS Provider Data (Nursing Homes)

Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS)

Monthly registry of certified skilled nursing and long-term care facilities, including licensed bed capacities and verified geographic coordinates.

Public & Maintained Open Data (data.cms.gov)
⚙️

Engineering Preprocessing & Data Quality Notice

While these sources are publicly maintained by federal agencies, they require careful pipeline preprocessing before ingestion into BigQuery. Government datasets contain known irregularities—such as inverted coordinate axes in certain regional records, blank-space string padding in place of true nulls, or omitted state reporting variables (e.g., Texas/Florida omitting LACKTRPT). Automated ETL scripts normalize, validate, and convert spatial geometries into BigQuery Geography prior to real-time spatial indexing.

📚 Complete Data Source Reference & Provenance

All datasets powering this evacuation readiness portal are authoritative, publicly maintained federal assets:

  1. FEMA National Shelter System (NSS): Maintained jointly by FEMA and the American Red Cross via gis.fema.gov.
  2. HHS emPOWER (County & ZIP): Managed by ASPR and CMS. Governed by strict privacy suppression (counts 1–10 masked as "11").
  3. U.S. Census Bureau ACS: Tract demographics natively stored in Google Cloud Public Datasets (bigquery-public-data.census_bureau_acs).
  4. CDC Social Vulnerability Index (SVI): Tract-level hazard resilience rankings by CDC/ATSDR.
  5. CDC PLACES: Small-area health estimates developed by CDC with the CDC Foundation (data.cdc.gov).
  6. FEMA National Risk Index (NRI): Quantitative multi-hazard annual loss and exposure index.
  7. CMS Provider Registry: Certified skilled nursing and health facility data on data.cms.gov.
